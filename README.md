## Overview

**Casbah Clean** is a 3D educational adventure game developed with Unity.
The game aims to raise awareness about environmental protection, waste sorting, recycling, and the preservation of cultural heritage.

The player controls **Mokrane**, a student from USTHB who decides to clean the polluted streets and houses of the Casbah of Algiers. Through different levels, the player collects waste, sorts it correctly, avoids obstacles, and helps restore the environment while progressing in the story.

## Project Context

This project was developed as part of the **Game Design and Virtual Reality** module.
The main theme of the project is **environmental awareness** through an interactive and educational game experience.

## Game Concept

The story takes place in the **Casbah of Algiers**, a historical district known for its narrow streets, traditional houses, stairs, balconies, and sea views.

The player must clean polluted areas, sort different types of waste, recycle correctly, and contribute to the renovation of houses. The progression of Mokrane’s house visually represents the positive impact of the player’s actions.

## Objectives

The main objectives of the game are to:

* Raise awareness about the impact of pollution on living spaces.
* Teach the basics of waste sorting and recycling.
* Encourage responsible environmental behavior.
* Highlight the importance of preserving cultural heritage.
* Show that small repeated actions can create a positive impact.

## Target Audience

The game is designed for:

* Students and young adults.
* Casual players.
* Families interested in ecology and heritage.
* Educational or associative environments.

## Gameplay

The game is structured into several short levels, each lasting around 4 to 5 minutes.

The player has to:

* Explore houses, streets, and green spaces.
* Collect different types of waste.
* Sort waste into the correct recycling bins.
* Avoid obstacles such as cars, animals, careless pedestrians, and broken objects.
* Manage score, time, and energy.
* Unlock the progression of Mokrane’s house after each level.

## Levels

### Level 0 – Story Introduction

This level introduces Mokrane, the Casbah, the pollution problem, and the main motivation behind the game.

### Level 1 – Tutorial and First House

The player learns the basic controls, movement, jumping, interaction, and waste collection.

### Level 2 – Streets of the Casbah

The player starts cleaning the streets while avoiding the first obstacles such as cars, animals, pedestrians, and objects on the ground.

### Level 3 – Woman’s House and Recycling

This level focuses on strict recycling.
The player must place each type of waste in the correct bin. Correct sorting gives a bonus, while wrong sorting may apply penalties.

### Level 4 – Final Cleaning Operation

The player cleans a large polluted green area and a very dirty house using upgraded equipment such as gloves, a shovel, a larger bag, and a cart.

## Waste Types

| Waste Type        | Base Score | Special Effect                              |
| ----------------- | ---------: | ------------------------------------------- |
| Plastic           |         10 | Energy bonus after collecting several items |
| Glass             |         12 | High score value                            |
| Paper / Cardboard |          8 | Common waste type                           |
| Biodegradable     |          5 | Used for specific level objectives          |
| Other Waste       |          3 | May cause penalty if wrongly sorted         |

## Victory and Defeat Conditions

### Victory

The player wins a level by reaching the required score before the end of the time limit while keeping enough energy.

### Defeat

The player loses if:

* The final score is below the required score.
* The energy reaches zero.
* The player fails to complete the objective in time.

## Educational Message

At the end of the game, the message is clear:

Pollution damages our streets, homes, health, and cultural heritage.
Everyone can help improve the situation by adopting small daily habits of cleanliness, respect, and responsibility.
